## 🌟 Why Lifevor?

- 🔒 **100% Private & Client-Side**: All computations execute directly inside your browser sandbox. Your data, code, spreadsheets, and metrics never touch external servers.
- ⚡️ **Instant & Frictionless**: Zero installation, zero queues, no forced logins, and no subscription paywalls. Open and calculate immediately.
- 🎯 **High Precision & Production-Ready**: From developer-grade hex/timestamp converters to scientific calculations, finance planning, and day-to-day utilities.
- 📱 **Adaptive & Accessible**: Crafted with an elegant responsive UI, seamless dark mode, and multi-language support (English & 中文).
